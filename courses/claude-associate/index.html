A study course for Anthropic's Claude Certified Associate – Foundations exam, built on its official blueprint: one module per exam domain, one session per objective. The exam

At a glance.

60questions
120minutes
720pass mark, on a 100–1,000 scale
$99exam fee (USD)

Multiple-choice and multiple-response questions (each says how many answers to pick), proctored online or at a Pearson VUE centre. No coding or API experience is needed: it's for people who use Claude to get business work done, such as operations, marketing, project management, education and communications. The credential lasts 12 months, with free online renewal.
