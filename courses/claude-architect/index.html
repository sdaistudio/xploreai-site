STUDY COURSE · CCAR-P · 7 MODULES · 38 SESSIONS

Claude Certified Architect – Professional.

A study course for Anthropic's Claude Certified Architect – Professional exam, built on its official blueprint: one module per exam domain. It's about decisions: which pattern, which model, which integration, which control, and how to justify each to engineers, security, legal and executives. Every idea comes as an animated architecture, a decision record or a trade-off worked through in Python that really runs, with practice questions to check yourself.

The exam

At a glance.

63questions
120minutes
720pass mark, on a 100–1,000 scale
$175exam fee (USD)

Multiple-choice and multiple-response questions (each says how many answers to pick), proctored online or at a Pearson VUE centre. It's the senior tier of the architect track, for solution architects, AI/ML engineers and technical leads who design and deliver production Claude systems end to end. Anthropic suggests three or more years in systems architecture or platform engineering and six months of production work with Claude or a similar LLM. There's no prerequisite. The credential lasts 12 months.
