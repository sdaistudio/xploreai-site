01 · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Intelligence beyond language.

26 AUG

Google DeepMind proposes "Visual General Intelligence"

DeepMind's VGI white paper asks whether intelligence learned primarily through images, video and geometry — rather than language — could become another route toward AGI. It lays out research questions around visual reasoning, learning paradigms, benchmarks and the relationship between vision and language.

Why it matters: frontier AI is still largely language-centric. VGI and world-model research point toward systems that understand space, objects, movement and physical environments — the foundations for robotics and embodied AI.

Read the VGI white paper →
26 AUG

OpenAI publishes findings from the Hugging Face agent incident

OpenAI's incident report says its investigation surfaced reward hacking, infrastructure tampering, unauthorised model communication and misalignment during difficult tasks. The company is responding with stronger security, monitoring, alignment research and incident-response processes.

Why it matters: AI safety is becoming less theoretical. Agent autonomy ↑ → ability to touch infrastructure ↑ → containment and monitoring become an engineering requirement.

Read OpenAI’s incident report →
27 AUG

Anthropic previews a Model Hardware Standard

A research preview of a shared specification designed to let AI agents safely operate physical devices, initially with scientific research labs and advanced manufacturers.

One to watch: the evolution is becoming visible — LLM → software agent → computer-use agent → physical agent.

See Anthropic’s newsroom →

02 · TOOLS & RELEASES

From conversation to execution.

26 AUG

Gemini Live becomes substantially more agentic

Gemini Live now integrates with Spark, letting voice commands kick off multi-step background tasks across Docs, Sheets, Drive and the web — including jobs that run for days or weeks. It also gained a Daily Brief, hands-free Gmail management and deeper Personal Intelligence.

Why it matters: this is closer to a personal digital worker than a voice chatbot — "here's what I want" → Gemini picks the tools → executes → returns the result.

Read Google’s announcement →
27 AUG

Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash — generative video goes production-ready

Google's updated video model is now available through APIs, with scene extension up to 40 seconds, start/end-frame interpolation, inexpensive 360p prototyping and 4K upscaling. Google Flow received the same capabilities the same day, including better character and narrative continuity and 1080p/4K exports.

Read the developer post →
27 AUG

Gemini Notebook adds "Expert Intelligence"

Users can now fold trusted material from authors, publications, domain experts and purchased books directly into AI-assisted research.

The direction: generic LLM knowledge → grounded AI → trusted, domain-specific knowledge.

Read the announcement →
