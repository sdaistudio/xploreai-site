01 · AI RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Proving, not just producing.

10 NOV · DEEPMIND

AlphaProof Code formally verifies Signal's cryptography

A multi-agent framework that mathematically verified key security properties of the production code behind the Signal protocol. Gemini Carbon-powered agents generated more than six million lines of Lean proofs — in about four weeks, versus what historically can take expert teams years.

Why it matters: AI is moving beyond finding bugs toward proving that critical software behaves correctly — an approach that could eventually cover banking software, medical devices and infrastructure.

10–12 NOV · OPENAI

Keeping increasingly capable models monitorable

New research on maintaining chain-of-thought monitorability across model training, and on automated red-teaming for fuzzy, dual-use areas such as biology and cybersecurity.

The shift: model safety → agent monitoring → automated red teams → continuous runtime controls — increasingly relevant as enterprise agents gain access to browsers, code execution, APIs and business systems.

See OpenAI’s alignment research →
9 NOV · ANTHROPIC

Claude reports major gains in science and engineering

During prerelease work, Anthropic says Claude Fable 5.2 / Mythos 5.2 helped design three functioning electronic devices, formalized Fermat's Last Theorem in roughly 30 hours, made progress on robotics tasks and solved two open black-hole physics problems — Anthropic-reported results, and treated as such.

The research signal: AI is moving from understanding scientific material toward designing, proving and experimentally validating things.

02 · AI MODELS & TOOLS

Agents gain economic authority.

🥇 9 NOV · ANTHROPIC

Claude Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2 launch

Substantial improvements across coding, computer use, scientific research, robotics and professional work: Fable 5.2 scores 69.9% on FrontierSWE v2 (vs 56.3% for 5.1), Mythos 5.2 reaches 75.3% on Terminal-Bench-Science, output is up to 30% faster, priced at $8/M input, $40/M output. The striking experiment: Fable 5.2 coordinated 1,000+ subagents for eight days to build a prototype operating system from an empty folder.

Why it matters: agent orchestration is becoming a genuine capability differentiator — not merely one model answering one prompt.

Read the announcement →
12 NOV · GOOGLE

Gemini 4 Hydrogen — the fast, cheap Gemini 4

Positioned as the cost-efficient Gemini 4 for coding and everyday agentic workflows, complementing deeper-reasoning Carbon and Argon: 1M-token context, $1.50/M input, $7.50/M output — scoring 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at default high thinking (vs 50 for Argon) while generally executing faster. Rolling out across AI Studio, the Gemini API, enterprise Gemini, the app and Search.

The market structure: frontier model for the hardest problems + faster/cheaper model for most agent steps — an architecture that will matter more to enterprise costs than standardizing on one LLM.

Read Google’s announcement →
💳 13 NOV · OPENAI

ChatGPT Wallet — agentic payments arrive

Initially for US Pro users through dots: agents can complete purchases after explicit user approval without ever receiving the card number, built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa. Today every transaction is reviewed and biometrically approved; the stated direction is pre-approved spending limits, categories and time periods.

The evolution: AI recommends → AI acts → AI transacts. For insurance and financial services, identity, authorization, fraud controls, audit trail and delegated spending authority become first-order design questions.

04 · ⚛️ QUANTUM — WEEKLY UPDATE

The strongest quantum week yet.

🥇 11 NOV · IBM

Nighthawk r3 — toward 1,000+ programmable qubits

The next Nighthawk processor is designed to execute 10,000 quantum operations across 520 programmable qubits, with two r3 processors to be connected in 2027 into a 1,040-qubit system. IBM is also integrating classical HPC directly into the IBM Quantum Platform; current r2 partners have executed 34+ billion circuits, with speedups up to 19× over earlier processors.

The metric shift: from "how many qubits?" to qubits × circuit depth × speed × fidelity × classical integration.

Read IBM’s announcement →
🤖⚛️ 12 NOV · Q-CTRL

Q-CTRL raises $290M for AI + Quantum convergence

A $290M Series C led by Salesforce Ventures makes Q-CTRL a quantum unicorn — described as the largest private financing yet for a software-focused quantum company. Its thesis: AI-driven control software that suppresses errors and noise in quantum hardware, plus quantum-navigation systems for GPS-denied environments.

A concrete example of the direction we track: AI → makes quantum hardware more useful.

Read Q-CTRL’s announcement →
12 NOV · AMD × PSIQUANTUM

AMD invests in PsiQuantum

An equity investment and expanded partnership combining PsiQuantum's photonic quantum technology with AMD CPUs, GPUs, adaptive computing and software — exploring Quantum + HPC + AI applications. One existing workflow already uses AMD GPUs in PsiQuantum's drug-discovery research to simulate covalent inhibitors relevant to oncology.

The architecture confirmed again: quantum will complement GPUs and classical HPC — not replace them.

Read the Reuters report →
13 NOV · ATOM COMPUTING

US backs Atom Computing with up to $100M

The neutral-atom quantum company finalized an agreement for up to $100M from the US Department of Commerce to accelerate fault-tolerant, utility-scale quantum computing. Atom already operates a 1,000+ qubit platform and says it is the only neutral-atom company in Stage C of DARPA's Quantum Benchmarking Initiative.

The bigger signal: governments are moving from funding basic research to funding manufacturing, logical qubits and commercialization.
