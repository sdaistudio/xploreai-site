01 · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

AI makes a validated discovery.

23 SEP

Claude discovers a novel enzyme system

Anthropic announced a new life-sciences research group and wet lab. In an early project, roughly 950 Claude agents searched DNA datasets for about 21 hours, generated hypotheses and — under high-level scientist direction — identified a previously uncharacterized enzyme system with CRISPR-like repeat properties (named ART, array-associated reverse transcriptases). Anthropic says the finding was then tested experimentally; the work is described in a preprint, with peer review pending.

Why it matters: the direction to watch in AI-for-science is literature assistant → hypothesis generator → research agent → experiment → validated discovery. The metric increasingly becomes discoveries per researcher, not benchmark accuracy.

02 · MODELS & TOOLS

The price of intelligence halves.

22 SEP · OPENAI

GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna launch

OpenAI expanded the GPT-6 family beyond Astra with two models aimed at different points on the cost–intelligence curve: Sol ($2 / $10 per 1M tokens) for high-capability professional work, and Luna ($0.10 / $0.50) for high-volume, cost-efficient intelligence — both 50% cheaper than their GPT-5.6 counterparts, and this time as default pricing, not promotional. Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 the same afternoon, about 90 minutes earlier.

The real story is cost per completed task: OpenAI reports Sol scoring 68.8% on DeepSWE 1.1 vs 69.9% for Claude Fable 5 at highest effort — at roughly 80% lower cost per task; Luna reaches 66.6% at ~96% less in that comparison. Don't ask only "which model is smartest?" Ask "what intelligence do I need at what cost for this task?"

THE ARCHITECTURE SHIFT

Model routing becomes the enterprise pattern

A practical enterprise architecture increasingly looks like: Luna → high-volume routine work · Sol → harder professional/agentic work · Astra → hardest frontier tasks. That model-routing strategy can materially change enterprise AI economics — from ₹/token to ₹/successful task, and eventually ₹/business outcome.

22 SEP · PALO ALTO NETWORKS

AI cybersecurity becomes a commercial service

Palo Alto Networks launched Unit 42 Continuous Frontier AI Defense, combining frontier models — including Claude Mythos 5 and GPT-5.6-Cyber — with open-weight models in a proprietary multi-model harness that continuously tests web applications, APIs and cloud infrastructure for vulnerabilities and suggests remediation. In internal deployment, it found a year's worth of exposures in three weeks.

Why it matters: AI now operates on both sides of cybersecurity — AI attacker ↔ AI defender — and defensive agents may become a major enterprise category.
