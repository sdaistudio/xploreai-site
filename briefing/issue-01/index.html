01 · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

From assistance to execution.

12 AUG

OpenAI: enterprises are delegating work, not just asking questions

New enterprise adoption research shows the shift from AI assistance toward AI execution. Codex generated 64% of combined ChatGPT + Codex output tokens among enterprise customers, and the most intensive "frontier" firms generated 8.3× as many tokens per active user as typical firms. Codex growth outside engineering was striking: weekly active enterprise users up 108× in legal, 41× in sales, 41× in recruiting and 26× in marketing since February.

Why it matters: concrete evidence that agentic AI is moving into real business workflows — well beyond IT.

14 AUG

Anthropic details text watermarking for Claude

Future Claude models will embed statistical watermarks in generated text, partly to comply with EU AI Act transparency requirements. Anthropic says the technique should not materially change output quality or token cost.

Why it matters: AI provenance is becoming part of the model architecture itself.
