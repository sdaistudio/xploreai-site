01 · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Safety becomes the constraint.

18 AUG

OpenAI slows frontier training over cyber risk

OpenAI disclosed it temporarily slowed frontier-model development — including a two-week pause in reinforcement-learning training and continued suspension of its largest planned frontier RL run. The trigger: a security incident plus preliminary evidence that its upcoming Astra model may reach a "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold. Monitoring some advanced workloads now consumes roughly 20% additional inference compute.

Why it matters: a frontier-AI milestone — security and alignment infrastructure may have to scale almost as fast as model intelligence itself.

Read OpenAI’s disclosure →
21 AUG

Anthropic publishes new interpretability research

Anthropic researchers introduced CHIVE, an agentic research pipeline that finds unexpected LLM behaviour and tests explanations through counterfactual prompt modifications. Notably, activation-reading interpretability tools did not improve agents' ability to predict experimental outcomes in this work. A separate Anthropic-affiliated study found fine-tuned AI lie detectors failed to generalize reliably to unfamiliar situations — larger prompted models often did better.

Why it matters: understanding and monitoring increasingly autonomous systems is now a research race of its own.

02 · TOOLS & RELEASES

The million-token era.

20 AUG

OpenRouter lists "Ox Alpha" — a free, million-token stealth model

The week's #1 trending AI item: Ox Alpha, a free stealth reasoning model aimed at coding, long-running agentic work and production workflows, with a huge 1,048,576-token context window. The provider remains undisclosed during the preview — treat circulating attribution claims as unconfirmed.

Why it matters: million-token context is moving from research curiosity to something developers can actually build agents around.

See Ox Alpha on OpenRouter →
19 AUG

Cerebras launches new wafer-scale inference hardware

Cerebras announced a new generation of its unusually large wafer-scale AI hardware and server system, aimed at dramatically accelerating chatbot and model inference — reinforcing the industry's emerging metric: cost per token → cost per completed task → intelligence per second.

Read the Reuters report →
19–20 AUG

Google: Gemma crosses 1 billion downloads; Search learns to teach

Google's open Gemma model family passed one billion downloads, and new AI-powered Search capabilities now help users learn concepts, study subjects and organise learning sessions. Also trending with developers this week: Claude Code's concise-output workflow and agent skills.

Tools signal: chatbot → coding copilot → long-context agent → persistent workflow executor.
