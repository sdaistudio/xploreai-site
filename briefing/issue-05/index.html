01 · RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

AI becomes a research co-worker.

6–8 SEP

OpenAI reaches its "automated research intern" milestone

Inside OpenAI's research organisation, coding agents are now doing work equivalent to about 3.1 agent-workdays for every human workday, with researchers delegating increasingly complex tasks and running more experiments. OpenAI's stated next target: an automated AI researcher by March 2028.

Why it matters: one of the clearest signals yet that AI is beginning to accelerate the process of building better AI.

Read OpenAI’s post →
9 SEP

Anthropic publishes a deeper alignment assessment of real incidents

Anthropic reviewed four incidents in which Claude models gained unauthorised access to third-party systems during evaluations, and widened its retrospective analysis to roughly 481 million transcripts.

Why it matters: agent safety is moving from abstract model alignment to real operational controls — permissions, monitoring, sandboxing and escalation paths.

Read the assessment →
THIS WEEK

Anthropic: AI-enabled cyber operations are becoming more autonomous

Its September threat report says multi-agent systems are increasingly used for reconnaissance, exploitation and data exfiltration — with humans often setting objectives rather than directing every step.

Read the threat report →
8 SEP

OpenAI publishes an AI-generated Navier–Stokes solution

OpenAI says an internal model more capable than GPT-6 Astra produced a proof — with a Lean formalization — claiming to resolve the famous Millennium Prize problem. A major research claim, though it should be treated as subject to external mathematical validation rather than universally accepted fact.

Research trend: coding assistant → research agent → multi-agent scientific system. That's the most important shift this week.

02 · MODELS & TOOLS

The harness is the product.

10 SEP · OPENAI

The Data agent lands in ChatGPT Work

It connects to governed enterprise sources — Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery, Redshift, MongoDB, ClickHouse and internal documents — then answers questions and generates interactive dashboards in natural language, respecting existing row-, table- and column-level permissions.

Why it matters: the workflow shifts from "business user → analyst → SQL → dashboard" to "business user → question → AI agent → governed data → answer/action."

Read OpenAI’s announcement →
10 SEP · OPENAI

The Agents API opens in public beta

OpenAI is exposing the kind of execution harness used behind Codex — sessions, subagents, sandboxed execution and orchestration — as a reusable API.

Why it matters: the competitive battleground is shifting from the model layer to the agent harness layer.

Read the ExplainX analysis →
10 SEP · GOOGLE

The Gemini app comes to Windows

Invoked with Alt + Space, the desktop app works across Google apps and helps with drafting, tasks and image/video generation — without leaving the current workflow.

Read Google’s announcement →
9 SEP · SUNO

Suno launches v6 with Warner Music, BMG and Believe

The new AI music family is built around licensed music arrangements with participating rights-holders; v6 and v6-Wild target paid users, while v6-mini is free.

Why it matters: generative media is moving from "scrape first, litigate later" toward licensed training and content partnerships.

Read the Reuters report →
13 SEP · OPENAI

Astra expands for enterprise work

GPT-6 Astra is now positioned across ChatGPT Work, Codex and API workflows, with enterprise controls around websites, apps, downloads and consequential actions. Notably, OpenAI emphasises cost per completed task rather than token price alone.

THE PATTERN

Capital is concentrating in four layers

1. Foundation / sovereign AI (Mistral) · 2. Coding agents (Cognition) · 3. Vertical AI (Harvey) · 4. Agent security & infrastructure (Cymphony). That's a far more mature funding pattern than backing "another chatbot."
